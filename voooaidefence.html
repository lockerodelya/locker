India's defence entrance examinations are among the most competitive and prestigious exams in the country. NDA (National Defence Academy) is conducted by UPSC twice a year for Class 12 passed candidates seeking entry into the Indian Army, Navy and Air Force. CDS (Combined Defence Services) is conducted by UPSC for graduates aspiring to become commissioned officers through IMA, INA, AFA and OTA. AFCAT (Air Force Common Admission Test) is conducted by the Indian Air Force for Flying Branch and Ground Duty branches. Each exam has a defined syllabus covering General Knowledge, English, Mathematics, Reasoning and Defence Studies. Beyond NDA and CDS, Indian defence recruitment includes several other important examinations. MNS (Military Nursing Service) selects nursing officers for the Indian Army. CAPF (Central Armed Police Forces) conducted by UPSC recruits Assistant Commandants for BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP and SSB. TA (Territorial Army) offers civilians a chance to serve the nation part-time alongside their profession. TES (Technical Entry Scheme) recruits Class 12 science students directly into the Indian Army as technical officers. ACC (Army Cadet Corps) and INET (Indian Navy Entrance Test) further expand the entry routes into the armed forces. Each of these exams demands rigorous daily practice across multiple subjects. 1NDA stands for:
A. National Defence Army
B. National Defence Academy
C. Naval Defence Academy
D. National Development Academy
Answer: B - National Defence Academy
The National Defence Academy (NDA) in Khadakwasla, Pune is the joint defence service training institute of the Indian Armed Forces. Cadets of all three services - Army, Navy and Air Force - train together.
2Who is the Supreme Commander of Indian Armed Forces?
A. Prime Minister
B. Defence Minister
C. President of India
D. Chief of Defence Staff
Answer: C - President of India
The President of India is the Supreme Commander of the Indian Armed Forces. However, actual executive authority over defence is exercised by the Cabinet Committee on Security headed by the Prime Minister.
3The Battle of Panipat was first fought in:
A. 1526
B. 1556
C. 1761
D. 1600
Answer: A - 1526
The First Battle of Panipat was fought on 21 April 1526 between Babur and Ibrahim Lodi. Babur's victory established the Mughal Empire in India. The second battle (1556) and third battle (1761) also took place at Panipat.
4Which is the highest military award in India?
A. Param Vir Chakra
B. Ashoka Chakra
C. Vir Chakra
D. Mahavir Chakra
Answer: A - Param Vir Chakra
The Param Vir Chakra (PVC) is India's highest wartime military decoration, awarded for the most conspicuous acts of bravery in the presence of the enemy. It was first awarded to Major Somnath Sharma in 1947.
5INS Vikramaditya is India's:
A. Nuclear submarine
B. Aircraft carrier
C. Destroyer
D. Frigate
Answer: B - Aircraft carrier
INS Vikramaditya is India's aircraft carrier, commissioned in 2013. It was originally the Soviet/Russian carrier Admiral Gorshkov, refitted at Sevmash shipyard. India now also has INS Vikrant, its first indigenous aircraft carrier.
6The Siachen Glacier dispute is between India and:
A. China
B. Nepal
C. Pakistan
D. Bangladesh
Answer: C - Pakistan
The Siachen Glacier in the Karakoram Range is the world's highest battleground. It has been disputed between India and Pakistan since 1984 when India launched Operation Meghdoot to secure the glacier.
7Which is the motto of the Indian Army?
A. Service Before Self
B. Touch the Sky with Glory
C. Sham No Varunah
D. May God Render the Sea Propitious
Answer: A - Service Before Self
The motto of the Indian Army is "Service Before Self." The Indian Air Force motto is "Touch the Sky with Glory" (Nabha Sparsham Deeptam). The Indian Navy motto is "Sham No Varunah" (May the Lord of Waters be auspicious to us).
8Kargil War between India and Pakistan took place in:
A. 1997
B. 1999
C. 2001
D. 2003
Answer: B - 1999
The Kargil War (Operation Vijay) was fought between India and Pakistan from May to July 1999 in the Kargil district of Jammu and Kashmir. India successfully recaptured its positions. Kargil Vijay Diwas is celebrated on 26 July.
9Which ocean does India's eastern coast border?
A. Arabian Sea
B. Indian Ocean
C. Bay of Bengal
D. Pacific Ocean
Answer: C - Bay of Bengal
India's eastern coast (Coromandel Coast) borders the Bay of Bengal. The western coast (Malabar Coast) borders the Arabian Sea. Both the Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea are parts of the Indian Ocean.
10The Agni missile is a type of:
A. Anti-aircraft missile
B. Ballistic missile
C. Cruise missile
D. Anti-tank missile
Answer: B - Ballistic missile
Agni is a series of Indian ballistic missiles developed by DRDO. Agni missiles are surface-to-surface ballistic missiles with ranges from 700 km (Agni-I) to over 5000 km (Agni-V), capable of carrying nuclear warheads.

1CDS exam is conducted by which body?
A. SSC
B. UPSC
C. IBPS
D. Ministry of Defence
Answer: B — UPSC
CDS (Combined Defence Services) examination is conducted by UPSC (Union Public Service Commission) twice a year. It selects candidates for IMA (Indian Military Academy), INA (Indian Naval Academy), AFA (Air Force Academy) and OTA (Officers Training Academy).
2India's first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) was:
A. Bipin Rawat
B. Anil Chauhan
C. V.K. Singh
D. Dalbir Singh Suhag
Answer: A — Bipin Rawat
General Bipin Rawat was appointed as India's first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) on 1 January 2020. The CDS post was created to bring about better integration among the three services. General Anil Chauhan became the second CDS after General Rawat's tragic demise in December 2021.
3The Strait of Malacca connects which two water bodies?
A. Red Sea and Arabian Sea
B. Pacific Ocean and Indian Ocean
C. Bay of Bengal and South China Sea
D. Indian Ocean and Atlantic Ocean
Answer: C — Bay of Bengal and South China Sea
The Strait of Malacca connects the Andaman Sea (Bay of Bengal) and the South China Sea (Pacific Ocean). It is one of the most important shipping lanes in the world, separating the Malay Peninsula from the Indonesian island of Sumatra. About 25% of world trade passes through it.
4Operation Vijay (1999) was related to:
A. Kashmir conflict
B. Kargil War
C. Bangladesh Liberation
D. Sri Lanka mission
Answer: B — Kargil War
Operation Vijay was launched by India in 1999 to recapture positions in the Kargil district of Jammu and Kashmir that had been occupied by Pakistani forces. The Indian Army successfully recaptured all positions by 26 July 1999. Kargil Vijay Diwas is celebrated on 26 July every year.
5The Battle of Plassey (1757) resulted in:
A. French gaining power
B. British gaining power in Bengal
C. Maratha victory
D. Dutch gaining power
Answer: B — British gaining power in Bengal
The Battle of Plassey on 23 June 1757 between the British East India Company and Siraj ud-Daulah (Nawab of Bengal) resulted in a decisive British victory, largely due to the treachery of Mir Jafar. This battle laid the foundation of British political dominance in India.
6Which is the highest gallantry award during peacetime in India?
A. Param Vir Chakra
B. Ashoka Chakra
C. Maha Vir Chakra
D. Kirti Chakra
Answer: B — Ashoka Chakra
The Ashoka Chakra is India's highest peacetime gallantry award, equivalent to the Param Vir Chakra for wartime. It is awarded for the most conspicuous bravery or self-sacrifice away from the battlefield. The Param Vir Chakra is the highest wartime gallantry award.
7The Siachen Glacier is part of which mountain range?
A. Himalayas
B. Karakoram
C. Hindu Kush
D. Pamirs
Answer: B — Karakoram
The Siachen Glacier is located in the Karakoram Range at an altitude of 5,400 metres. It is the world's highest battlefield. India has been maintaining its position on Siachen since Operation Meghdoot in April 1984. It is strategically important for controlling the Saltoro Ridge.
8INS Vikrant (2022) is significant because:
A. It is India's first nuclear submarine
B. It is India's first indigenous aircraft carrier
C. It is India's fastest destroyer
D. It is India's first guided missile cruiser
Answer: B — India's first indigenous aircraft carrier
INS Vikrant, commissioned on 2 September 2022, is India's first indigenously designed and built aircraft carrier. It was constructed at Cochin Shipyard Limited. With this, India became one of the few countries capable of designing and building an aircraft carrier.
9The Nobel Peace Prize 2014 was jointly awarded to:
A. Modi and Obama
B. Malala Yousafzai and Kailash Satyarthi
C. Ban Ki-moon and Angela Merkel
D. Obama and Mandela
Answer: B — Malala Yousafzai and Kailash Satyarthi
The 2014 Nobel Peace Prize was jointly awarded to Malala Yousafzai (Pakistan) and Kailash Satyarthi (India) for their struggle against suppression of children and for the right of all children to education. Kailash Satyarthi founded the Bachpan Bachao Andolan movement.
10Which Article of Indian Constitution deals with the Prime Minister?
A. Article 52
B. Article 74
C. Article 75
D. Article 79
Answer: C — Article 75
Article 75 of the Indian Constitution deals with the appointment and other conditions relating to the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers. It states that the Prime Minister shall be appointed by the President, and other Ministers shall be appointed by the President on the advice of the Prime Minister.
