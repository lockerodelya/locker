🗺️ KVS Geography
1Which is the largest ocean in the world?
A. Atlantic Ocean
B. Indian Ocean
C. Arctic Ocean
D. Pacific Ocean
Answer: D — Pacific Ocean
The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth, covering more than 165 million square kilometres. It accounts for about 46% of the world's total ocean surface.
2The Deccan Plateau is made up of which rock?
A. Limestone
B. Sandstone
C. Basalt
D. Granite
Answer: C — Basalt
The Deccan Plateau is composed mainly of basalt, formed by massive volcanic eruptions about 66 million years ago. These volcanic rocks are called Deccan Traps and are rich in black soil.
3Which is the highest peak in the world?
A. K2
B. Kangchenjunga
C. Mount Everest
D. Lhotse
Answer: C — Mount Everest
Mount Everest at 8,848.86 metres is the highest mountain on Earth. Located in the Himalayas on the border of Nepal and Tibet, it was first summited by Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay in 1953.
4The Amazon rainforest is located in which continent?
A. Africa
B. South America
C. Asia
D. Australia
Answer: B — South America
The Amazon Rainforest is located primarily in Brazil, South America. It is the world's largest tropical rainforest, covering about 5.5 million square kilometres and housing 10% of all species on Earth.
5Which Indian state receives highest annual rainfall?
A. Kerala
B. Assam
C. Meghalaya
D. West Bengal
Answer: C — Meghalaya
Meghalaya receives the highest average annual rainfall in India. Mawsynram in Meghalaya holds the record for the highest average rainfall in the world at approximately 11,871 mm per year.
6The Sahara Desert is located in which continent?
A. Asia
B. Australia
C. Africa
D. South America
Answer: C — Africa
The Sahara Desert in North Africa is the world's largest hot desert, covering about 9.2 million square kilometres across 11 countries. It spans from the Atlantic coast to the Red Sea.
7Which is the longest mountain range in the world?
A. Himalayas
B. Andes
C. Rockies
D. Alps
Answer: B — Andes
The Andes is the world's longest continental mountain range, stretching approximately 7,000 km along the western coast of South America. It passes through seven countries including Peru and Chile.
8The Western Ghats run parallel to which coast?
A. Eastern coast
B. Northern border
C. Western coast
D. Southern tip
Answer: C — Western coast
The Western Ghats (Sahyadri range) run parallel to the western coast of India for about 1,600 km. They are a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a global biodiversity hotspot.
9International Date Line passes through which ocean?
A. Atlantic Ocean
B. Indian Ocean
C. Arctic Ocean
D. Pacific Ocean
Answer: D — Pacific Ocean
The International Date Line is an imaginary line at approximately 180° longitude that passes through the Pacific Ocean. Crossing it going east subtracts a day; going west adds a day.
10Which is the smallest country in the world?
A. Monaco
B. San Marino
C. Vatican City
D. Liechtenstein
Answer: C — Vatican City
Vatican City, with an area of about 0.44 square kilometres, is the smallest country in the world. It is an independent city-state enclaved within Rome, Italy, and is the headquarters of the Catholic Church.
