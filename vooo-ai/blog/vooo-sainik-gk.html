⚔️ Sainik School General Knowledge
1Who was the first Prime Minister of India?
A. Sardar Patel
B. Jawaharlal Nehru
C. Rajendra Prasad
D. B.R. Ambedkar
Answer: B — Jawaharlal Nehru
Jawaharlal Nehru was the first Prime Minister of independent India, serving from 15 August 1947 until his death on 27 May 1964. He is also known as Pandit Nehru and Chacha Nehru.
2The national bird of India is:
A. Peacock
B. Parrot
C. Sparrow
D. Eagle
Answer: A — Peacock
The Indian Peacock (Pavo cristatus) is the national bird of India. It was designated the national bird in 1963. The peacock is known for its beautiful iridescent plumage and is found across the Indian subcontinent.
3India got independence in which year?
A. 1942
B. 1945
C. 1947
D. 1950
Answer: C — 1947
India gained independence from British rule on 15 August 1947. The Indian Independence Act was passed by the British Parliament, partitioning British India into two dominions: India and Pakistan.
4Which is the capital city of India?
A. Mumbai
B. Kolkata
C. Chennai
D. New Delhi
Answer: D — New Delhi
New Delhi is the capital of India and seat of all three branches of the Government of India. It is located within the National Capital Territory of Delhi and became the capital in 1911.
5The Indian Army Day is celebrated on:
A. 15 August
B. 26 January
C. 15 January
D. 7 December
Answer: C — 15 January
Indian Army Day is celebrated on 15 January every year. On this day in 1949, General K.M. Cariappa became the first Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army, taking over from British General Sir Francis Butcher.
6Who wrote the Indian National Anthem?
A. Bankim Chandra
B. Rabindranath Tagore
C. Sarojini Naidu
D. Mahatma Gandhi
Answer: B — Rabindranath Tagore
Jana Gana Mana was written and composed by Rabindranath Tagore. It was officially adopted as the National Anthem of India on 24 January 1950. Its full version takes approximately 52 seconds to sing.
7Mount Everest is located in which country?
A. India
B. China
C. Nepal
D. Bhutan
Answer: C — Nepal
Mount Everest, the world's highest peak at 8,848.86 metres, lies on the border of Nepal and Tibet (China). The peak itself is on the Nepalese side. It was first climbed in 1953.
8How many states are there in India?
A. 25
B. 28
C. 29
D. 27
Answer: B — 28
India currently has 28 states and 8 Union Territories. The most recent change was in 2019 when Jammu & Kashmir was reorganised into two Union Territories: Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh.
9The Rajya Sabha is also called:
A. Upper House
B. Lower House
C. People's House
D. State Assembly
Answer: A — Upper House
The Rajya Sabha is the upper house of India's Parliament. It has a maximum of 250 members, of whom 12 are nominated by the President. The Lok Sabha is the lower house or People's House.
10Which river flows through the city of Delhi?
A. Ganga
B. Brahmaputra
C. Yamuna
D. Chambal
Answer: C — Yamuna
The Yamuna River flows through Delhi. It is the longest tributary of the Ganga and originates from Yamunotri Glacier in Uttarakhand. Delhi's major monuments including the Red Fort stand along the Yamuna.
