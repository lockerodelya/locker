📜 Indian History (Q1–Q20)
1Who founded the Indian National Congress in 1885?
A. Bal Gangadhar Tilak
B. A.O. Hume
C. Dadabhai Naoroji
D. Gopal Krishna Gokhale
Answer: B — A.O. Hume
Allan Octavian Hume, a retired British civil servant, founded the Indian National Congress in 1885 in Bombay.
2The Battle of Plassey (1757) was fought between the British East India Company and whom?
A. Hyder Ali
B. Tipu Sultan
C. Siraj ud-Daulah
D. Mir Qasim
Answer: C — Siraj ud-Daulah
The Battle of Plassey was fought between Robert Clive of the British East India Company and Siraj ud-Daulah, the Nawab of Bengal.
3Which movement was launched by Mahatma Gandhi in 1942 demanding an end to British rule?
A. Non-Cooperation Movement
B. Civil Disobedience Movement
C. Quit India Movement
D. Swadeshi Movement
Answer: C — Quit India Movement
Gandhi launched the Quit India Movement on 8 August 1942, demanding an immediate end to British rule in India.
4The Jallianwala Bagh massacre took place in which year?
A. 1915
B. 1917
C. 1919
D. 1921
Answer: C — 1919
The Jallianwala Bagh massacre occurred on 13 April 1919 when British troops under General Dyer fired on a peaceful gathering in Amritsar.
5Who was the first Governor-General of independent India?
A. Lord Mountbatten
B. C. Rajagopalachari
C. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
D. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
Answer: A — Lord Mountbatten
Lord Mountbatten served as the first Governor-General of independent India from August 1947 to June 1948.
6The Dandi March of 1930 was a protest against which British policy?
A. Rowlatt Act
B. Salt Tax
C. Partition of Bengal
D. Arms Act
Answer: B — Salt Tax
Gandhi led the 241-mile Dandi March to protest the British monopoly on salt and the heavy salt tax imposed on Indians.
7Which dynasty built the famous Brihadeeswarar Temple in Thanjavur?
A. Pallava
B. Hoysala
C. Chola
D. Pandya
Answer: C — Chola
The Brihadeeswarar Temple was built by Raja Raja Chola I and completed in 1010 CE. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
8Akbar's policy of religious tolerance was called:
A. Sulh-i-Kul
B. Din-i-Ilahi
C. Ibadat Khana
D. Mansabdari
Answer: B — Din-i-Ilahi
Din-i-Ilahi (Divine Faith) was a syncretic religion propounded by Akbar in 1582, incorporating elements from Islam, Hinduism, Zoroastrianism and Christianity.
9The Simon Commission was appointed in 1927 to review which act?
A. Indian Councils Act 1909
B. Government of India Act 1919
C. Rowlatt Act 1919
D. Morley-Minto Reforms 1909
Answer: B — Government of India Act 1919
The Simon Commission was set up to review the working of the Government of India Act 1919 and suggest further constitutional reforms.
10Who among the following was NOT a member of the Constituent Assembly of India?
A. B.R. Ambedkar
B. Jawaharlal Nehru
C. Mahatma Gandhi
D. Rajendra Prasad
Answer: C — Mahatma Gandhi
Mahatma Gandhi was not a member of the Constituent Assembly. He was assassinated on 30 January 1948, before the Constitution was completed.

🌍 Indian Geography (Q11–Q30)
11Which is the longest river in India?
A. Ganga
B. Godavari
C. Brahmaputra
D. Yamuna
Answer: A — Ganga
The Ganga is the longest river in India at approximately 2,525 km. The Indus is longer but most of it flows through Pakistan.
12The Tropic of Cancer passes through how many Indian states?
A. 6
B. 8
C. 9
D. 7
Answer: B — 8
The Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram.
13Which state has the longest coastline in India?
A. Tamil Nadu
B. Maharashtra
C. Gujarat
D. Andhra Pradesh
Answer: C — Gujarat
Gujarat has the longest coastline among all Indian states at approximately 1,600 km due to its irregular peninsular shape.
14The Western Ghats is also known as:
A. Vindhya Range
B. Sahyadri
C. Satpura Range
D. Aravalli Range
Answer: B — Sahyadri
The Western Ghats is also known as Sahyadri. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the world's eight biodiversity hotspots.
15Where is the Kaziranga National Park located?
A. Assam
B. Uttarakhand
C. Madhya Pradesh
D. West Bengal
Answer: A — Assam
Kaziranga National Park is located in Assam and is famous for the Indian one-horned rhinoceros. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
⚖️ Indian Polity (Q16–Q35)
16The Constitution of India came into force on:
A. 15 August 1947
B. 26 November 1949
C. 26 January 1950
D. 15 August 1950
Answer: C — 26 January 1950
The Constitution of India was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into force on 26 January 1950, celebrated as Republic Day.
17Which article of the Indian Constitution abolishes untouchability?
A. Article 14
B. Article 15
C. Article 17
D. Article 21
Answer: C — Article 17
Article 17 of the Indian Constitution abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form.
18The President of India is elected by:
A. Lok Sabha members only
B. All members of Parliament
C. Elected members of Parliament and State Legislatures
D. All citizens of India
Answer: C — Elected members of Parliament and State Legislatures
The President is elected by an Electoral College consisting of elected members of both Houses of Parliament and elected members of State Legislative Assemblies.
19How many Fundamental Rights are guaranteed by the Indian Constitution?
A. 5
B. 6
C. 7
D. 9
Answer: B — 6
The Constitution originally guaranteed 7 Fundamental Rights. The Right to Property was removed in 1978 by the 44th Amendment, leaving 6 Fundamental Rights.
20Which schedule of the Indian Constitution deals with anti-defection laws?
A. Eighth Schedule
B. Ninth Schedule
C. Tenth Schedule
D. Eleventh Schedule
Answer: C — Tenth Schedule
The Tenth Schedule, added by the 52nd Constitutional Amendment in 1985, contains provisions relating to disqualification on grounds of defection.
💰 Indian Economy (Q21–Q40)
21Which body presents the Union Budget of India?
A. Reserve Bank of India
B. Planning Commission
C. Ministry of Finance
D. NITI Aayog
Answer: C — Ministry of Finance
The Union Budget is presented by the Finance Minister on behalf of the Ministry of Finance, typically on 1 February each year.
22GST in India was implemented on:
A. 1 April 2017
B. 1 July 2017
C. 1 January 2018
D. 1 April 2016
Answer: B — 1 July 2017
Goods and Services Tax (GST) was implemented in India on 1 July 2017, replacing multiple indirect taxes with a unified tax system.
23NITI Aayog replaced which body?
A. Planning Commission
B. Finance Commission
C. Election Commission
D. Economic Advisory Council
Answer: A — Planning Commission
NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India) was established on 1 January 2015, replacing the Planning Commission which had existed since 1950.
24The headquarters of the Reserve Bank of India is in:
A. New Delhi
B. Mumbai
C. Kolkata
D. Chennai
Answer: B — Mumbai
The Reserve Bank of India was established on 1 April 1935 and its central office (headquarters) is located in Mumbai.
25Which five-year plan focused on heavy industries and was called the Mahalanobis Plan?
A. First Five Year Plan
B. Second Five Year Plan
C. Third Five Year Plan
D. Fourth Five Year Plan
Answer: B — Second Five Year Plan
The Second Five Year Plan (1956-61), based on the Mahalanobis model, focused on rapid industrialization with emphasis on heavy industries.
🔬 General Science (Q26–Q45)
26Which gas is known as laughing gas?
A. Carbon dioxide
B. Nitrogen
C. Nitrous oxide
D. Oxygen
Answer: C — Nitrous oxide
Nitrous oxide (N₂O) is commonly known as laughing gas because inhaling it can cause euphoria and laughter. It is also used as an anaesthetic.
27The speed of light in vacuum is approximately:
A. 3 × 10⁸ cm/s
B. 3 × 10⁸ m/s
C. 3 × 10⁶ m/s
D. 3 × 10¹⁰ m/s
Answer: B — 3 × 10⁸ m/s
The speed of light in vacuum is approximately 299,792,458 metres per second, commonly approximated as 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
28DNA stands for:
A. Deoxyribose Nucleic Acid
B. Deoxyribonucleic Acid
C. Diribonucleic Acid
D. Dinucleic Acid
Answer: B — Deoxyribonucleic Acid
DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid) is the molecule that carries genetic information in all living organisms and many viruses.
29Which vitamin is produced by the human body when exposed to sunlight?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B12
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin D
Answer: D — Vitamin D
Vitamin D is synthesized in the skin when it is exposed to ultraviolet B (UVB) rays from sunlight. It is essential for calcium absorption.
30The chemical formula of common salt is:
A. KCl
B. NaCl
C. CaCl₂
D. MgCl₂
Answer: B — NaCl
Sodium Chloride (NaCl) is the chemical name for common table salt. It is an ionic compound made of sodium and chlorine ions.
🌿 Environment & Ecology (Q31–Q50)
31The Kyoto Protocol is related to:
A. Nuclear disarmament
B. Reduction of greenhouse gas emissions
C. Biodiversity conservation
D. Marine pollution
Answer: B — Reduction of greenhouse gas emissions
The Kyoto Protocol (1997) is an international treaty that commits its parties to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, based on the principle that global warming exists.
32Which gas is primarily responsible for the greenhouse effect?
A. Oxygen
B. Nitrogen
C. Carbon dioxide
D. Hydrogen
Answer: C — Carbon dioxide
Carbon dioxide (CO₂) is the primary greenhouse gas emitted through human activities. It traps heat in the atmosphere causing global warming.
33Project Tiger was launched in India in which year?
A. 1973
B. 1980
C. 1968
D. 1992
Answer: A — 1973
Project Tiger was launched on 1 April 1973 under Prime Minister Indira Gandhi to protect the Bengal tiger and its habitat across India.
34The ozone layer is found in which layer of the atmosphere?
A. Troposphere
B. Stratosphere
C. Mesosphere
D. Thermosphere
Answer: B — Stratosphere
The ozone layer is concentrated in the lower portion of the stratosphere, approximately 15-35 km above Earth's surface. It absorbs UV radiation.
35Chipko Movement was primarily associated with:
A. Water conservation
B. Forest conservation
C. Wildlife protection
D. Soil conservation
Answer: B — Forest conservation
The Chipko Movement of the 1970s in Uttarakhand was a non-violent protest where villagers hugged trees to prevent deforestation by contractors.
📰 Current Affairs 2025–2026 (Q36–Q50)
36India's first Bullet Train corridor is being built between which two cities?
A. Mumbai and Ahmedabad
B. Delhi and Mumbai
C. Chennai and Bangalore
D. Delhi and Kolkata
Answer: A — Mumbai and Ahmedabad
The Mumbai-Ahmedabad High Speed Rail Corridor is India's first bullet train project, being built with Japanese technology and funding.
37Which Indian city hosted the G20 Summit in 2023?
A. New Delhi
B. Mumbai
C. Bengaluru
D. Varanasi
Answer: A — New Delhi
India hosted the G20 Summit on 9-10 September 2023 in New Delhi under the theme "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — One Earth, One Family, One Future".
38India's Chandrayaan-3 mission successfully landed on which part of the Moon?
A. North Pole
B. South Pole
C. Equatorial region
D. Far side
Answer: B — South Pole
Chandrayaan-3 made India the first country to land near the lunar south pole on 23 August 2023, a historic achievement for ISRO.
39The Digital India initiative was launched in which year?
A. 2013
B. 2014
C. 2015
D. 2016
Answer: C — 2015
Digital India was launched on 1 July 2015 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi with the vision to transform India into a digitally empowered society.
40Which Indian received the Nobel Prize in Economics in 2019?
A. Abhijit Banerjee
B. Amartya Sen
C. Raghuram Rajan
D. Kaushik Basu
Answer: A — Abhijit Banerjee
Abhijit Banerjee (along with Esther Duflo and Michael Kremer) won the 2019 Nobel Prize in Economics for their experimental approach to alleviating global poverty.
