NDA Exam 2026 - Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about NDA preparation and how Vooo AI helps you clear it.

What is the NDA exam pattern 2026?
NDA and NA Examination has two papers. Mathematics Paper: 300 marks, 120 questions, 150 minutes. General Ability Test (GAT): 600 marks, 150 questions, 150 minutes. GAT covers English (200 marks) and General Knowledge (400 marks including Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Polity and Current Affairs). Negative marking of one-third applies. Total written examination is 900 marks.
Who is eligible for NDA exam 2026?
Candidates who have passed or are appearing in Class 12 with Physics and Mathematics from a recognised board are eligible. Age limit is 16.5 to 19.5 years at the time of course commencement. Both male and female candidates are eligible. UPSC conducts NDA twice a year — NDA 1 in April and NDA 2 in September.
Is NDA Maths very difficult?
NDA Maths is Class 11 and 12 CBSE standard covering Algebra, Trigonometry, Calculus, Matrices, Vectors and Statistics. It is manageable for students who studied Mathematics at Class 12 level. However, the time pressure of 75 seconds per question requires consistent daily practice. Vooo AI unlimited Maths MCQ practice builds this calculation speed with fresh questions every day.
What happens after clearing NDA written exam?
Candidates who clear NDA written are called for SSB (Services Selection Board) Interview — a 5-day assessment by Army, Navy or Air Force. SSB tests Officer Like Qualities, personality, leadership and intelligence. The written exam and SSB together determine final NDA merit for joining the National Defence Academy at Khadakwasla, Pune.
How many questions should I practice daily for NDA?
For NDA preparation, 40 to 60 MCQs per day split between Maths and GAT is ideal. Vooo AI free plan gives 30 fresh MCQs daily — ideal for early preparation. Paid plan gives unlimited access, ideal for the final 60 to 90 day intensive sprint where solving 80 to 100 questions across both papers daily is recommended.
Can I prepare for NDA without coaching?
Yes. Many NDA successful candidates prepare through self-study using NCERT books for Class 11 and 12, standard reference books for GAT, and consistent daily MCQ practice. Vooo AI provides the daily practice component — the most critical self-study element. Regular practice directly builds the Maths speed and GAT accuracy needed to clear NDA written examination.
