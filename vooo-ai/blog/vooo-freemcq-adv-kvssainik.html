Frequently Asked Questions

KVS, Sainik School, JNV & NTSE — Questions Answered

Everything parents and students want to know about using Vooo AI to prepare for these prestigious school entrance exams.

My child is appearing for KVS Class 1 admission — can Vooo AI help?
Yes. Vooo AI's Foundation-level MCQs for Class 1 are designed for children aged 5–6 — covering number recognition, simple counting, basic shapes, matching and pattern completion at an age-appropriate level. These build the early number sense and reasoning skills that KVS Class 1 admission tests assess.
How is the JNV JNVST mental ability test different from regular reasoning MCQs?
The JNVST Class 6 Mental Ability section is entirely figure-based — no language, no numbers, just geometric figures and spatial patterns. Many students from rural backgrounds find this unfamiliar and challenging. Vooo AI specifically generates these non-verbal, figure-pattern MCQs — Odd Figure Out, Pattern Completion, Figure Matrices, Mirror Images and Paper Folding — which are the actual JNVST Mental Ability question types.
How many Sainik Schools are there and does Vooo AI cover all of them?
There are 33 Sainik Schools across India under the Sainik Schools Society, Ministry of Defence — from Sainik School Chittorgarh (Rajasthan) to Sainik School Bijapur (Karnataka), Sainik School Tilaiya (Jharkhand) and others. All follow the centrally conducted AISSEE pattern. Vooo AI's MCQs are mapped to the AISSEE syllabus and are therefore relevant for admission to every Sainik School in India.
What is the difference between NTSE Stage 1 and Stage 2 and does Vooo AI cover both?
NTSE Stage 1 is a state-level exam conducted by each state board — difficulty varies by state but follows the NCERT standard. Stage 2 is the national exam conducted by NCERT — significantly harder, especially the MAT section. Vooo AI generates MCQs at both difficulty levels — Intermediate (Stage 1 standard) and Advanced (Stage 2 standard) — allowing students to progressively prepare for both stages.
Can a student prepare for KVS, Sainik School and NTSE simultaneously on Vooo AI?
Yes — and this is one of Vooo AI's great strengths. The core skills tested across all four exams overlap significantly: Maths, Mental Ability, Reasoning and Pattern Recognition are common to KVS, Sainik, JNV and NTSE. A student practicing 30 MCQs daily across these categories is simultaneously preparing for all four exams, saving time and coaching costs.
Is Vooo AI useful for RIMC Rashtriya Indian Military College admission preparation?
Yes. RIMC Dehradun is a prestigious military school admitting students at Class 8 level. The RIMC entrance exam tests Mathematics, English, General Knowledge and Intelligence — all of which are covered by Vooo AI's Maths and Logical Reasoning MCQ categories. RIMC preparation sits naturally alongside Sainik School Class 9 preparation on the platform.
