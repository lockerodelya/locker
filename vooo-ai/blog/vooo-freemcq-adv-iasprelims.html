Frequently Asked Questions

IAS Prelims Preparation — Your Questions Answered

Common questions from IAS aspirants about Prelims preparation and how Vooo AI helps.

What is the IAS Prelims exam pattern in 2026?
IAS Prelims 2026 consists of two papers. GS Paper 1 has 100 questions for 200 marks in 120 minutes — covering History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Environment, Science and Current Affairs. CSAT Paper 2 has 80 questions for 200 marks in 120 minutes — it is a qualifying paper (minimum 33% required). Both papers have negative marking of one-third per wrong answer.
How many candidates appear for IAS Prelims and how many clear it?
Approximately 5 to 6 lakh candidates appear for IAS Prelims every year. Only around 10,000 to 15,000 (roughly 2–3%) qualify for Mains. This makes Prelims the most competitive stage of the UPSC journey — where daily MCQ practice volume makes the decisive difference between clearing and not clearing.
Which subject is most important for IAS Prelims?
Current Affairs consistently contributes 20–25 questions — the highest single contribution in GS Paper 1. History (15–20 questions) and Indian Polity (12–15 questions) are the next most important. However, all subjects are important since the cut-off is often decided by 3–5 questions. A balanced preparation across all topics is the most reliable strategy.
How many MCQs should I practice daily for IAS Prelims?
For IAS Prelims preparation, practicing 50–100 MCQs per day is the recommended target for aspirants in the 6–12 month preparation phase. Vooo AI's free plan gives 30 fresh MCQs daily — ideal for early preparation. Can I crack IAS Prelims without joining a coaching institute?
Yes — many IAS toppers have cleared Prelims through self-study. The key ingredients are: NCERT books for the foundation, standard reference books for each subject, a daily newspaper for current affairs, and consistent daily MCQ practice for speed and accuracy. Vooo AI provides the daily MCQ practice component — the one aspect that most self-study aspirants lack access to in sufficient volume.
