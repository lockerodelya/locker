🎖️ RIMC Entrance GK
1RIMC (Rashtriya Indian Military College) is located in:
A. Delhi
B. Pune
C. Dehradun
D. Secunderabad
Answer: C — Dehradun
RIMC is located in Dehradun, Uttarakhand. Established in 1922 as Prince of Wales Royal Indian Military College, it was renamed RIMC after independence. Boys from Class 8 join and study until Class 12, after which they are directly eligible for NDA (National Defence Academy) entry. It is considered the cradle of India's military leadership.
2The National Defence Academy (NDA) is located in:
A. Delhi
B. Pune
C. Khadakwasla
D. Dehradun
Answer: C — Khadakwasla
The National Defence Academy (NDA) is located at Khadakwasla, near Pune, Maharashtra. It is a joint services academy where cadets of all three services — Army, Navy and Air Force — train together for three years before going to their respective service academies. NDA is considered one of the finest military academies in the world.
3India's first Prime Minister was:
A. Sardar Patel
B. Dr. Rajendra Prasad
C. Jawaharlal Nehru
D. C. Rajagopalachari
Answer: C — Jawaharlal Nehru
Jawaharlal Nehru was India's first Prime Minister, serving from 15 August 1947 until his death on 27 May 1964 — the longest-serving PM of India. He was a key architect of modern India, establishing its democratic institutions, IITs, ISRO and IIMs. His birthday 14 November is celebrated as Children's Day.
4Which river is the longest in India?
A. Yamuna
B. Godavari
C. Ganga
D. Narmada
Answer: C — Ganga
The Ganga (Ganges) is the longest river in India at about 2,525 km. It originates at Gangotri Glacier (Gaumukh) in Uttarakhand and flows eastward through UP, Bihar and West Bengal before joining the Bay of Bengal. The Indus, though associated with India, is longer overall but most of it now flows through Pakistan.
5The chemical symbol of Gold is:
A. Go
B. Gd
C. Au
D. Ag
Answer: C — Au
Gold's chemical symbol is Au, from the Latin word "Aurum" meaning gold. Silver = Ag (Argentum), Iron = Fe (Ferrum), Copper = Cu (Cuprum), Lead = Pb (Plumbum), Mercury = Hg (Hydrargyrum), Sodium = Na (Natrium), Potassium = K (Kalium). These Latin-based symbols are important for chemistry and general science.
6Mount Everest, the world's highest peak, is in:
A. India
B. China only
C. Nepal, on the Nepal-China border
D. Tibet only
Answer: C — Nepal, on the Nepal-China border
Mount Everest (8,849 metres) sits on the border between Nepal (where it is called Sagarmatha) and Tibet, China (where it is called Chomolungma). Edmund Hillary (New Zealand) and Tenzing Norgay Sherpa (Nepal) made the first confirmed ascent on 29 May 1953. Bachendri Pal was the first Indian woman to climb Everest (1984).
7The Indian Army's highest peacetime gallantry award is:
A. Param Vir Chakra
B. Ashoka Chakra
C. Vir Chakra
D. Kirti Chakra
Answer: B — Ashoka Chakra
The Ashoka Chakra is India's highest peacetime gallantry award, equivalent to the Param Vir Chakra which is awarded during wartime. The Param Vir Chakra (PVC) is the highest wartime gallantry award. The order of gallantry awards: PVC, MVC (Maha Vir Chakra), VrC (Vir Chakra) for wartime; Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, Shaurya Chakra for peacetime.
8The Tropic of Cancer passes through which of these Indian states?
A. Kerala
B. Rajasthan
C. Tamil Nadu
D. Karnataka
Answer: B — Rajasthan
The Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N) passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram. It does not pass through Kerala, Tamil Nadu or Karnataka (all south of the Tropic). Mnemonic: "Gujarati Rajput Meets Chhattisgarhis Just When Tribals Marvel."
9Which of these is India's indigenously developed fighter aircraft?
A. Mirage 2000
B. Su-30MKI
C. Tejas
D. Rafale
Answer: C — Tejas
Tejas (LCA — Light Combat Aircraft) is India's indigenously designed and manufactured single-engine multirole fighter aircraft, developed by HAL (Hindustan Aeronautics Limited) and ADA (Aeronautical Development Agency). It entered service with the IAF in 2016. Tejas Mark 1A is the upgraded version currently being produced.
10The Indian Navy's motto is:
A. Service Before Self
B. Sham No Varunah
C. Touch the Sky with Glory
D. In Service of the Nation
Answer: B — Sham No Varunah
"Sham No Varunah" is the Indian Navy's motto, a Sanskrit phrase from the Vedas meaning "May the Lord of the Waters (Varuna) be auspicious unto us." The Indian Army's motto is "Service Before Self." The Indian Air Force's motto is "Touch the Sky with Glory" (Nabh Sprisham Deeptam from the Bhagavad Gita). The Coast Guard's motto is "Vayam Rakshamah" (We Protect).
