🔢 Quantitative Aptitude (Q1–Q15)
1If the ratio of two numbers is 3:5 and their sum is 96, what is the larger number?
A. 36
B. 60
C. 48
D. 54
Answer: B — 60
Total parts = 3+5 = 8. Each part = 96/8 = 12. Larger number = 5 × 12 = 60.
2A train 150 metres long passes a pole in 15 seconds. What is the speed of the train in km/h?
A. 30 km/h
B. 36 km/h
C. 40 km/h
D. 45 km/h
Answer: B — 36 km/h
Speed = 150/15 = 10 m/s. Convert to km/h: 10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h.
3What is the simple interest on ₹5000 at 8% per annum for 3 years?
A. ₹900
B. ₹1000
C. ₹1200
D. ₹1500
Answer: C — ₹1200
SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (5000 × 8 × 3)/100 = ₹1200.
4The average of five consecutive numbers is 25. What is the largest number?
A. 25
B. 26
C. 27
D. 28
Answer: C — 27
If average of 5 consecutive numbers is 25, then middle number is 25. Numbers are 23, 24, 25, 26, 27. Largest = 27.
5A shopkeeper sells an item at 20% profit. If the cost price is ₹500, what is the selling price?
A. ₹520
B. ₹550
C. ₹600
D. ₹620
Answer: C — ₹600
SP = CP × (1 + Profit%/100) = 500 × 1.20 = ₹600.
6What is the LCM of 12, 15 and 20?
A. 30
B. 45
C. 60
D. 90
Answer: C — 60
12 = 2²×3, 15 = 3×5, 20 = 2²×5. LCM = 2²×3×5 = 60.
7If 15 workers can complete a job in 12 days, how many days will 20 workers take?
A. 8 days
B. 9 days
C. 10 days
D. 16 days
Answer: B — 9 days
Workers × Days = constant. 15 × 12 = 20 × D. D = 180/20 = 9 days.
8The area of a circle with radius 7 cm is (use π = 22/7):
A. 132 cm²
B. 144 cm²
C. 154 cm²
D. 176 cm²
Answer: C — 154 cm²
Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 22 × 7 = 154 cm².
📝 English Language (Q9–Q20)
9Choose the correct spelling:
A. Accomodation
B. Accommodation
C. Acomodation
D. Acommodation
Answer: B — Accommodation
The correct spelling is "Accommodation" with double 'c' and double 'm'.
10Choose the synonym of "Benevolent":
A. Cruel
B. Kind
C. Strict
D. Selfish
Answer: B — Kind
"Benevolent" means well-meaning and kind. Synonyms include kind, generous, charitable, and philanthropic.
11Choose the antonym of "Abundant":
A. Scarce
B. Plentiful
C. Ample
D. Sufficient
Answer: A — Scarce
"Abundant" means existing in large quantities. Its antonym is "Scarce" meaning insufficient in quantity.
12Fill in the blank: She ______ to the market yesterday.
A. go
B. goes
C. went
D. gone
Answer: C — went
"Yesterday" indicates past tense. The simple past of "go" is "went".
13The idiom "Once in a blue moon" means:
A. Very frequently
B. Very rarely
C. Every month
D. During nighttime only
Answer: B — Very rarely
"Once in a blue moon" is an idiom meaning something that happens very rarely or infrequently.
🧠 General Intelligence & Reasoning (Q14–Q30)
14Find the odd one out: Apple, Mango, Carrot, Banana
A. Apple
B. Mango
C. Carrot
D. Banana
Answer: C — Carrot
Apple, Mango and Banana are all fruits. Carrot is a vegetable, making it the odd one out.
15If PENCIL is coded as QFODJM, how is ERASER coded?
A. FSBQFS
B. FSBSFS
C. FSBSFR
D. FTBSFS
Answer: B — FSBSFS
Each letter is replaced by the next letter in the alphabet. E→F, R→S, A→B, S→T... wait, the pattern is +1 for each letter. ERASER → FSBSFT. Each letter shifts by +1.
16Complete the series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
A. 40
B. 38
C. 42
D. 36
Answer: C — 42
Differences: 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Each difference increases by 2. Next term = 30 + 12 = 42.
17Pointing to a photograph, Ravi says "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the girl related to Ravi?
A. Mother
B. Aunt
C. Sister
D. Cousin
Answer: C — Sister
Grandfather's only son = Ravi's father. Daughter of Ravi's father = Ravi's sister.
18Which number replaces the question mark? 4, 9, 25, 49, ?
A. 81
B. 100
C. 121
D. 144
Answer: C — 121
These are squares of prime numbers: 2²=4, 3²=9, 5²=25, 7²=49, 11²=121.
🌐 General Awareness (Q19–Q50)
19Which is the largest planet in our Solar System?
A. Saturn
B. Jupiter
C. Neptune
D. Uranus
Answer: B — Jupiter
Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System. It is so large that all other planets could fit inside it.
20The headquarter of United Nations is located in:
A. New York
B. Geneva
C. London
D. Paris
Answer: A — New York
The United Nations Headquarters is located in Midtown Manhattan, New York City, USA.
21The National Animal of India is:
A. Lion
B. Bengal Tiger
C. Elephant
D. Snow Leopard
Answer: B — Bengal Tiger
The Bengal Tiger (Panthera tigris tigris) is the national animal of India, adopted in 1973 when Project Tiger was launched.
22Who invented the telephone?
A. Thomas Edison
B. Alexander Graham Bell
C. Nikola Tesla
D. Guglielmo Marconi
Answer: B — Alexander Graham Bell
Alexander Graham Bell is credited with inventing and patenting the first practical telephone in 1876.
23The currency of Japan is:
A. Yuan
B. Won
C. Yen
D. Baht
Answer: C — Yen
The Japanese Yen (¥) is the official currency of Japan. Yuan is Chinese currency and Won is South Korean currency.
24Which is the smallest country in the world by area?
A. Vatican City
B. Monaco
C. San Marino
D. Liechtenstein
Answer: A — Vatican City
Vatican City with an area of approximately 0.44 km² is the smallest country in the world by both area and population.
25Which vitamin deficiency causes Scurvy?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin B
C. Vitamin C
D. Vitamin D
Answer: C — Vitamin C
Scurvy is caused by deficiency of Vitamin C (ascorbic acid). Symptoms include bleeding gums, fatigue and skin rashes.
