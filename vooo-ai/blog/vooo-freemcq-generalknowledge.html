UPSC General Knowledge MCQ Practice

Essential GK questions covering Geography, Economy, Science, Polity and Current Affairs

These essential MCQ questions are selected from the most important topics in UPSC Civil Services Prelims. Sections include Geography, Indian Economy, General Science, Indian Polity and Current Affairs. UPSC Civil Services Examination is the most prestigious and intellectually demanding competitive examination in India, conducted by the Union Public Service Commission to select candidates for the Indian Administrative Service, Indian Foreign Service, Indian Police Service and dozens of other Group A and Group B Central Services, and building a comprehensive, multi-dimensional and deeply analytical preparation across every subject of the Prelims syllabus is the single most important requirement for every aspirant who dreams of joining the civil services. Geography is one of the most expansive and consistently important subjects in UPSC Civil Services Prelims, covering both Indian and World Geography across physical, human, economic and environmental dimensions. Physical Geography topics such as the interior structure of the earth, plate tectonics and earthquakes, volcanoes, types of rocks and rock cycle, weathering and erosion, landforms created by rivers, glaciers, wind and sea waves, atmospheric circulation, pressure belts and wind systems, ocean currents, tides, coral reefs and the distribution of biomes across the world are fundamental areas that UPSC aspirants must master with conceptual clarity. Indian Geography covers the physiographic divisions of India including the Himalayan ranges, the Northern Plains, the Peninsular Plateau, the Thar Desert, the Coastal Plains and the Island groups of Andaman and Nicobar and Lakshadweep. The drainage system of India, including the Himalayan river systems of the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra and the Peninsular river systems of the Godavari, Krishna, Cauvery, Mahanadi and Narmada, their tributaries, river basin characteristics and inter-linking river projects are critically important geography topics in UPSC Prelims MCQs. Climate of India, the mechanism of the Indian monsoon, distribution of rainfall, types of soils, natural vegetation zones, forest types and their distribution, wildlife sanctuaries and national parks, biosphere reserves, Ramsar wetlands and UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India are all high-priority topics that appear with great regularity in Civil Services Prelims question papers. Economic Geography covering the distribution of minerals, energy resources, agricultural patterns, industrial regions, transport networks and population distribution across India and the world forms another essential component of UPSC Geography preparation. Indian Economy is one of the most dynamic, complex and heavily weighted subjects in UPSC Civil Services Prelims, requiring aspirants to develop a strong understanding of both foundational economic concepts and the latest policy developments shaping India's economic landscape. Fundamental macroeconomic concepts such as GDP, GNP, NNP, national income measurement, inflation and its types, monetary policy and fiscal policy, money supply and credit creation, the role and functions of the Reserve Bank of India, commercial banking, non-banking financial companies and the structure of India's financial markets are essential building blocks of UPSC economy preparation. The Union Budget, its components, types of deficits including fiscal deficit, revenue deficit and primary deficit, government borrowing, public debt management and the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act are important topics regularly tested in Prelims. Agriculture and rural economy topics including the Green Revolution, land reforms, agricultural credit, minimum support price, crop insurance schemes like PMFBY, food security and the Public Distribution System, and the role of NABARD and cooperative banks in rural financing are critical areas for UPSC economy MCQs. India's industrial policy evolution, the role of public sector enterprises, the liberalisation privatisation and globalisation reforms of 1991, foreign direct investment and foreign portfolio investment, special economic zones, the Make in India initiative, the Production Linked Incentive schemes and India's integration into global value chains are important topics connecting economic history with current policy in UPSC Prelims. International trade, India's balance of payments, the current account and capital account, exchange rate systems, the role of the World Trade Organisation, International Monetary Fund, World Bank Group, Asian Development Bank and the New Development Bank are important international economy topics regularly featured in Civil Services Prelims questions. Indian Polity and Constitution is one of the most systematically structured and rewarding subjects in the UPSC Prelims syllabus. The making of the Indian Constitution, its sources and salient features, the Preamble and its significance, Fundamental Rights under Part III, Directive Principles of State Policy under Part IV, Fundamental Duties, the amendment procedure under Article 368 and important constitutional amendments from the First Amendment to the most recent ones are absolutely foundational topics for every UPSC aspirant. The structure and functioning of the Union Executive including the President, Vice President, Prime Minister and Council of Ministers, the Parliament including Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha, their composition powers and procedures, the legislative process, Parliamentary committees and the role of the Speaker and Chairman are critical Polity topics tested extensively in Prelims. The Union Judiciary including the Supreme Court, its original appellate and advisory jurisdiction, judicial review and judicial activism, the system of Public Interest Litigation and the appointment and removal of judges are important topics that connect Polity with current affairs. Federalism in India, the distribution of legislative powers between the Union and States, the Concurrent List, Centre-State financial relations, the Finance Commission, the role of the Governor, State Legislature and High Courts, and the constitutional provisions for President's Rule under Article 356 are important federal structure topics in UPSC Polity MCQs. Local self-government institutions, the 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments establishing Panchayati Raj institutions and Urban Local Bodies, their composition, powers and functions and the role of State Finance Commissions and State Election Commissions are topics that connect constitutional provisions with grassroots governance. Constitutional bodies like the Election Commission, Comptroller and Auditor General, Union Public Service Commission, National Commissions for SC ST Women and Minorities, and statutory and non-statutory bodies like NITI Aayog, Planning Commission, National Human Rights Commission and Central Information Commission are important Polity topics regularly tested in Prelims. General Science in UPSC Prelims covers Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Space Technology, Defence Technology, Biotechnology and Science in Everyday Life, requiring aspirants to develop both conceptual understanding and awareness of the latest scientific and technological developments shaping India and the world. Environment and Ecology has emerged as one of the most important and fastest-growing sections of UPSC Civil Services Prelims in recent years. Topics such as biodiversity and its conservation, ecosystem structure and function, environmental pollution and its mitigation, climate change and India's commitments under the Paris Agreement, important environmental conventions and protocols, environmental impact assessment, forest rights and tribal issues, and the functioning of regulatory bodies like the National Green Tribunal are critical areas that every UPSC aspirant must prepare with great thoroughness and attention to current developments. Current Affairs for UPSC Civil Services Prelims demands consistent daily reading and analysis of national and international events across governance, economy, science, environment, security, foreign policy and social issues. 🌍 UPSC General Knowledge
1Which planet is closest to the Sun?
A. Venus
B. Mercury
C. Mars
D. Earth
Answer: B - Mercury
Mercury is the smallest and innermost planet in the Solar System. Its orbit around the Sun takes 87.97 Earth days, the shortest of all planets.
2Tropic of Cancer passes through which Indian state?
A. Kerala
B. Tamil Nadu
C. Gujarat
D. Karnataka
Answer: C - Gujarat
The Tropic of Cancer (23.5°N) passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram.
3India's First Five Year Plan began in:
A. 1951
B. 1947
C. 1956
D. 1950
Answer: A - 1951
India's First Five Year Plan was launched in 1951 under PM Jawaharlal Nehru. It focused on agriculture and was based on the Harrod-Domar model.
4Which gas is most abundant in Earth's atmosphere?
A. Oxygen
B. Nitrogen
C. Carbon dioxide
D. Argon
Answer: B - Nitrogen
Nitrogen makes up approximately 78% of Earth's atmosphere, followed by Oxygen at 21%, Argon at 0.93% and Carbon dioxide at 0.04%.
5Who is called the Father of Indian Constitution?
A. Jawaharlal Nehru
B. B.R. Ambedkar
C. Mahatma Gandhi
D. Sardar Patel
Answer: B - B.R. Ambedkar
Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was the chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Indian Constitution. He is widely regarded as the Father of the Indian Constitution.
6Which river is called the Sorrow of Bihar?
A. Ganga
B. Gandak
C. Kosi
D. Son
Answer: C - Kosi
The Kosi River is called the Sorrow of Bihar due to its frequent devastating floods. It originates in Nepal and joins the Ganga in Bihar.
7GST in India was implemented in which year?
A. 2016
B. 2017
C. 2018
D. 2015
Answer: B - 2017
Goods and Services Tax (GST) was implemented in India on 1 July 2017, replacing a complex system of central and state taxes with a unified indirect tax structure.
8Which vitamin is produced by sunlight on skin?
A. Vitamin A
B. Vitamin C
C. Vitamin K
D. Vitamin D
Answer: D - Vitamin D
Vitamin D is synthesised in human skin upon exposure to ultraviolet B (UVB) radiation from sunlight. It is essential for calcium absorption and bone health.
9Sundarbans delta is formed by which rivers?
A. Ganga and Yamuna
B. Ganga and Brahmaputra
C. Brahmaputra and Barak
D. Mahanadi and Godavari
Answer: B - Ganga and Brahmaputra
The Sundarbans is the world's largest mangrove forest, formed by the delta of the Ganga, Brahmaputra and Meghna rivers. It spans India and Bangladesh.
10Which Article of Constitution abolishes untouchability?
A. Article 14
B. Article 16
C. Article 17
D. Article 21
Answer: C - Article 17
Article 17 of the Indian Constitution abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form. Enforcement of any disability arising out of untouchability is an offence.
