🦁 Maharashtra Geography & GK
1The capital of Maharashtra is:
A. Pune
B. Nagpur
C. Mumbai
D. Nashik
Answer: C — Mumbai
Mumbai is the capital of Maharashtra and the financial capital of India. It is India's most populous city and home to the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), National Stock Exchange (NSE) and Bollywood — the Hindi film industry. Mumbai's Dharavi was once Asia's largest slum.
2Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj founded which empire?
A. Mughal Empire
B. Maratha Empire
C. Vijayanagara Empire
D. Bahmani Sultanate
Answer: B — Maratha Empire
Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj founded the Maratha Empire in 1674, becoming its first Chhatrapati (Emperor). He was a master of guerrilla warfare and built a strong navy. He is revered as a great Hindu king and the Father of the Indian Navy. His coronation took place at Raigad Fort.
3The Deccan Plateau in Maharashtra is formed by:
A. River deposits
B. Volcanic lava flows
C. Wind deposits
D. Glacial deposits
Answer: B — Volcanic lava flows
The Deccan Plateau in Maharashtra is formed by ancient volcanic lava flows called Deccan Traps (Deccan Basalt), which occurred about 66 million years ago. The black cotton soil (Regur) derived from this basalt is extremely fertile and ideal for growing cotton, making Maharashtra a major cotton producer.
4The Ajanta Caves in Maharashtra are famous for:
A. Rock-cut temples of Shaivism
B. Buddhist paintings and sculptures
C. Jain temples
D. Medieval fortifications
Answer: B — Buddhist paintings and sculptures
The Ajanta Caves (near Aurangabad) are 30 rock-cut Buddhist caves dating from the 2nd century BCE to 480 CE. They contain magnificent paintings and sculptures depicting the life of Buddha and Jataka tales. They were declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983.
5Which river flows through Pune?
A. Godavari
B. Krishna
C. Mula-Mutha
D. Tapi
Answer: C — Mula-Mutha
The Mula and Mutha rivers flow through Pune and merge to form the Mula-Mutha river. Pune is also called the Oxford of the East for its educational institutions. The city is home to the Aga Khan Palace where Mahatma Gandhi and Kasturba Gandhi were imprisoned during the Quit India Movement.
6The Gateway of India is located in which city?
A. Pune
B. Nashik
C. Mumbai
D. Aurangabad
Answer: C — Mumbai
The Gateway of India is an arch monument built in Mumbai to commemorate the landing of King George V and Queen Mary in December 1911. It was completed in 1924. Ironically, the last British troops to leave India after Independence passed through this gateway in February 1948.
7Which district of Maharashtra is known as the Wine Capital of India?
A. Nashik
B. Pune
C. Kolhapur
D. Sangli
Answer: A — Nashik
Nashik is called the Wine Capital of India, accounting for over 60% of India's wine production. The region's climate and soil are ideal for grape cultivation. Nashik is also one of the four cities that hosts the Kumbh Mela and is known for its Pandharpur pilgrimage route.
8The Ellora Caves near Aurangabad contain temples of:
A. Only Buddhism
B. Only Hinduism
C. Buddhism, Hinduism and Jainism
D. Only Jainism
Answer: C — Buddhism, Hinduism and Jainism
The Ellora Caves (34 rock-cut monasteries and temples) represent Buddhist (caves 1-12), Hindu (caves 13-29) and Jain (caves 30-34) traditions, built between 600-1000 CE. The Kailasa Temple (Cave 16), a monolithic Hindu temple carved from a single rock, is the largest rock-cut structure in the world.
9Lavani is a traditional dance form of which state?
A. Gujarat
B. Rajasthan
C. Maharashtra
D. Karnataka
Answer: C — Maharashtra
Lavani is a traditional folk dance form of Maharashtra, performed to the rhythm of dholki (drum). It combines song and dance and is known for its energy and erotic themes. Tamasha is the folk theatre form in which Lavani is performed. It is an important part of Maharashtra's cultural heritage.
10The Koyna Dam is built on which river in Maharashtra?
A. Krishna
B. Koyna
C. Godavari
D. Bhima
Answer: B — Koyna
The Koyna Dam (Shivaji Sagar Lake) is built on the Koyna River in Satara district, Maharashtra. It is the largest dam in Maharashtra and one of the largest in India. The Koyna Hydroelectric Project is Maharashtra's most important power source. The region experienced a devastating earthquake in 1967.
