🔬 General Science (Q1–Q25)
1What is the chemical symbol for Iron?
A. Ir
B. Fe
C. In
D. I
Answer: B — Fe
The chemical symbol for Iron is Fe, derived from the Latin word "Ferrum".
2The unit of electric current is:
A. Volt
B. Watt
C. Ampere
D. Ohm
Answer: C — Ampere
The SI unit of electric current is Ampere (A), named after André-Marie Ampère. Volt measures voltage, Watt measures power, Ohm measures resistance.
3Which gas is used in electric bulbs?
A. Oxygen
B. Argon
C. Nitrogen
D. Hydrogen
Answer: B — Argon
Argon gas is filled in electric bulbs to prevent the tungsten filament from burning out quickly. It is an inert gas that does not react with the filament.
4The human body has how many bones?
A. 196
B. 200
C. 206
D. 212
Answer: C — 206
An adult human body has 206 bones. Babies are born with around 270-300 bones, which fuse together as they grow.
5Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
A. Venus
B. Mars
C. Jupiter
D. Saturn
Answer: B — Mars
Mars is called the Red Planet because its surface is covered with iron oxide (rust), giving it a reddish appearance.
6Sound travels fastest through:
A. Air
B. Water
C. Steel
D. Vacuum
Answer: C — Steel
Sound travels fastest through solids because molecules are packed closely together. In steel, sound travels at about 5,100 m/s compared to 343 m/s in air.
7Photosynthesis occurs in which part of the plant?
A. Root
B. Stem
C. Leaves
D. Flower
Answer: C — Leaves
Photosynthesis primarily occurs in leaves, specifically in the chloroplasts which contain chlorophyll that captures sunlight for energy conversion.
8What is the boiling point of water at sea level?
A. 90°C
B. 95°C
C. 100°C
D. 105°C
Answer: C — 100°C
Water boils at 100°C (212°F) at standard atmospheric pressure (1 atm) at sea level.
9The nearest star to Earth (after the Sun) is:
A. Proxima Centauri
B. Sirius
C. Alpha Centauri
D. Betelgeuse
Answer: A — Proxima Centauri
Proxima Centauri is the nearest star to our Solar System at about 4.24 light years away. It is part of the Alpha Centauri star system.
10Which blood group is known as the universal donor?
A. O negative
B. AB positive
C. A positive
D. B negative
Answer: A — O negative
O negative blood group is the universal donor because it can be donated to patients of all blood groups. AB positive is the universal recipient.

🔢 Mathematics (Q11–Q25)
11What is 15% of 240?
A. 30
B. 32
C. 36
D. 40
Answer: C — 36
15% of 240 = (15/100) × 240 = 0.15 × 240 = 36.
12The square root of 1444 is:
A. 36
B. 37
C. 38
D. 39
Answer: C — 38
38 × 38 = 1444. So √1444 = 38.
13A can do a piece of work in 10 days. B can do the same work in 15 days. In how many days can they do it together?
A. 5 days
B. 6 days
C. 8 days
D. 12 days
Answer: B — 6 days
A's work per day = 1/10, B's work per day = 1/15. Together = 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6. So they finish in 6 days.
14The perimeter of a square with side 9 cm is:
A. 27 cm
B. 36 cm
C. 45 cm
D. 81 cm
Answer: B — 36 cm
Perimeter of square = 4 × side = 4 × 9 = 36 cm.
15What is the HCF of 36 and 48?
A. 6
B. 9
C. 12
D. 18
Answer: C — 12
Factors of 36: 1,2,3,4,6,9,12,18,36. Factors of 48: 1,2,3,4,6,8,12,16,24,48. HCF = 12.
🌐 General Knowledge (Q16–Q30)
16Indian Railways is under which ministry?
A. Ministry of Transport
B. Ministry of Railways
C. Ministry of Infrastructure
D. Ministry of Commerce
Answer: B — Ministry of Railways
Indian Railways is managed by the Ministry of Railways of the Government of India. It is one of the largest railway networks in the world.
17The first railway line in India was laid between:
A. Bombay to Thane
B. Calcutta to Delhi
C. Madras to Bangalore
D. Delhi to Agra
Answer: A — Bombay to Thane
India's first railway line was opened on 16 April 1853 between Bori Bunder (Bombay/Mumbai) and Thane, covering a distance of 34 km.
18The headquarter of Railway Board is in:
A. New Delhi
B. Mumbai
C. Kolkata
D. Chennai
Answer: A — New Delhi
The Railway Board, the apex body that manages Indian Railways, is headquartered in Rail Bhawan, New Delhi.
19Vande Bharat Express is which type of train?
A. Diesel engine train
B. Semi-high speed electric train
C. Maglev train
D. Solar powered train
Answer: B — Semi-high speed electric train
Vande Bharat Express is a semi-high speed, self-propelled electric train set designed and manufactured in India. It can reach speeds up to 160 km/h.
20How many railway zones are there in India?
A. 14
B. 16
C. 18
D. 21
Answer: C — 18
Indian Railways is divided into 18 zones. The latest zone added was South Coast Railway (SCoR) headquartered in Visakhapatnam.
21Which is the longest railway platform in India?
A. Kharagpur
B. Hubballi (Hubli)
C. Gorakhpur
D. Allahabad
Answer: B — Hubballi (Hubli)
Hubballi (Hubli) railway station in Karnataka has the longest railway platform in India at 1,505 metres, surpassing the earlier record holder Gorakhpur.
22The Konkan Railway connects which two cities?
A. Roha to Mangaluru
B. Mumbai to Goa
C. Pune to Bangalore
D. Chennai to Kochi
Answer: A — Roha to Mangaluru
Konkan Railway runs from Roha in Maharashtra to Mangaluru in Karnataka, covering 741 km through the scenic Konkan coast.
23IRCTC stands for:
A. Indian Rail Corporation and Tourism Committee
B. Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation
C. Indian Railways Central Ticketing Committee
D. Indian Rail Commerce and Travel Corporation
Answer: B — Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation
IRCTC is a subsidiary of Indian Railways that manages online ticketing, catering and tourism services.
24Diesel Locomotive Works (DLW) is located in:
A. Kolkata
B. Chennai
C. Varanasi
D. Pune
Answer: C — Varanasi
Diesel Locomotive Works (DLW) is located in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. It manufactures diesel and electric locomotives for Indian Railways.
25The Rajdhani Express was first introduced in which year?
A. 1969
B. 1972
C. 1975
D. 1980
Answer: A — 1969
The first Rajdhani Express ran between New Delhi and Howrah on 3 March 1969. It was India's first superfast train with air-conditioned coaches.
