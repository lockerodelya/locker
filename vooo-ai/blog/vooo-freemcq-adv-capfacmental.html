CAPF AC Exam 2026 - Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about CAPF preparation and how Vooo AI helps you clear the written exam.

What is the CAPF AC exam pattern 2026?
CAPF AC exam conducted by UPSC has two written papers. Paper 1 is objective GS (250 marks, 125 questions, 2 hours 30 minutes) covering General Studies, Mental Ability, Quantitative Aptitude and Current Affairs. Paper 2 is descriptive (200 marks, 3 hours) covering Essay, Comprehension and General Studies analytical questions. After written exam, shortlisted candidates appear for Physical Test, Medical Exam and Personality Test (150 marks). Total final selection is based on written exam and personality test.
Who is eligible for CAPF AC exam 2026?
Candidates with a graduation degree from a recognised university are eligible. Age limit is 20 to 25 years (with relaxation for SC/ST/OBC candidates as per government rules). Both male and female candidates are eligible. For BSF and ITBP, candidates must be medically fit for high altitude service. UPSC conducts CAPF AC exam once a year.
Which forces are covered under CAPF?
CAPF includes five forces: Border Security Force (BSF) — India's largest border guarding force; Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) — India's largest paramilitary force; Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) — guards airports, nuclear plants and public sector undertakings; Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) — guards the China border in Himalayan terrain; and Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) — guards Nepal and Bhutan borders.
Is CAPF AC easier than IAS?
CAPF AC is considered less competitive than IAS Civil Services but still highly demanding. The GS Paper 1 covers a similar syllabus to IAS Prelims. However, CAPF has Paper 2 descriptive instead of IAS Mains. The selection process is faster and CAPF offers immediate posting as a gazetted officer in paramilitary service. For those seeking a government officer career without the multi-year IAS preparation journey, CAPF is an excellent alternative.
