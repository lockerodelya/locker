Frequently Asked Questions

UPSC CSAT Paper 2 — Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about CSAT preparation and how Vooo AI helps you clear the qualifying barrier.

What is CSAT Paper 2 and why is it important?
CSAT (Civil Services Aptitude Test) Paper 2 is the second paper of the UPSC Prelims examination. It tests Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Data Interpretation, Analytical Ability, Decision Making and Reading Comprehension. It is qualifying in nature — you must score at least 33% (66.66 marks out of 200). If you fail to qualify, your GS Paper 1 marks are not counted, regardless of how well you performed.
How many questions are in CSAT Paper 2 and what is the time limit?
CSAT Paper 2 has 80 questions for 200 marks, to be completed in 120 minutes. Each correct answer gives 2.5 marks. Each wrong answer carries a negative marking of 0.833 marks (one-third of 2.5). Decision Making questions carry no negative marking. This gives you approximately 90 seconds per question — making speed and accuracy training critically important.
Which topics should I focus most on for CSAT Paper 2?
Based on previous year question papers, Reading Comprehension (20–25 questions) and Logical Reasoning (15–20 questions) together account for nearly half the paper. Quantitative Aptitude (15–20 questions) is where most aspirants lose marks. Prioritise Comprehension for quick wins, then Reasoning, then Maths. Decision Making questions (5–10) carry no negative marking — always attempt them all.
How many CSAT MCQs should I practice per day?
For aspirants with 3–6 months to Prelims, practicing 30–50 CSAT MCQs per day is ideal. Vooo AI's free plan gives you 30 unique MCQs daily. Paid plan users get unlimited access — ideal for intensive 100-day CSAT crash preparation where solving 80–100 questions daily is recommended.
Can I clear CSAT without a coaching institute?
Absolutely. CSAT is a skills-based paper, not a knowledge-based one. The skills it tests — speed calculation, logical deduction, comprehension — improve through consistent daily practice, not classroom teaching. Many UPSC toppers have cleared CSAT through self-study and regular MCQ practice alone. Vooo AI provides the practice volume that makes self-study CSAT preparation fully possible.
Does CSAT preparation also help in UPSC Mains?
Yes. The Analytical Ability and Reading Comprehension skills developed through CSAT preparation directly improve your UPSC Mains Essay and General Studies answer quality. Decision Making practice builds ethical reasoning that is directly tested in GS Paper 4. Strong CSAT preparation creates a foundation that benefits every stage of the UPSC journey.
