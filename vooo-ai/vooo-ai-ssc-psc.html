What is the difference between NDA and CDS on Vooo AI?
NDA is for Class 12 pass/appearing students (unmarried males and females) and has a dedicated Mathematics paper at Class 11–12 level plus a GAT (General Ability Test). CDS is for graduates and has Elementary Mathematics at Class 10 level plus an English and GK paper. Both are conducted by UPSC. Vooo AI covers both at their respective difficulty levels.
